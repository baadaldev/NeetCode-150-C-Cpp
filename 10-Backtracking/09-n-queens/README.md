## 📌 Problem Overview
- **Topic:** Backtracking
- **LeetCode ID:** [51 - N-Queens](https://leetcode.com/problems/n-queens/)
- **Difficulty:** 🔴 Hard
- **Time Complexity:** `O(n!)`
- **Space Complexity:** `O(n)`

---

## 💡 Algorithmic Approach & Summary
Track occupied columns, positive diagonals (r + c), and negative diagonals (r - c) to place n non-attacking queens.
