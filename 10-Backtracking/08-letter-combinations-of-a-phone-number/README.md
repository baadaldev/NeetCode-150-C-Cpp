## 📌 Problem Overview
- **Topic:** Backtracking
- **LeetCode ID:** [17 - Letter Combinations of a Phone Number](https://leetcode.com/problems/letter-combinations-of-a-phone-number/)
- **Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium
- **Time Complexity:** `O(4^n * n)`
- **Space Complexity:** `O(n)`

---

## 💡 Algorithmic Approach & Summary
Map phone digits 2-9 to letters, then backtrack for each digit's mapped characters.
