## 📌 Problem Overview
- **Topic:** Backtracking
- **LeetCode ID:** [131 - Palindrome Partitioning](https://leetcode.com/problems/palindrome-partitioning/)
- **Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium
- **Time Complexity:** `O(n * 2^n)`
- **Space Complexity:** `O(n)`

---

## 💡 Algorithmic Approach & Summary
At each index, find all palindromic prefixes and recurse on the remainder of the string.
